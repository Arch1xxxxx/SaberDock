# SaberDock 1.0.2

Organize custom Beat Saber maps on your Mac and sync playlists to Apple Vision Pro using your own AVP Play game files.

- Drag-and-drop map imports and folder-based playlists.
- Song covers, supported map colors, and available difficulties.
- Song removal, restoration, ordering, and sorting.
- One-click headset library updates and custom app names.
- English and Simplified Chinese interfaces.

**Prepare at least two nonempty playlists before installing or updating. Each needs at least one supported map. A single playlist cannot reach the headset main menu.**

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later, Xcode with a compatible visionOS SDK, a paired Vision Pro, and a completed Beat Saber 1.40.8 installation through AVP Play, with its matching visionOS app build retained on your Mac. SaberDock reuses that app; it does not translate or rebuild the game executable.

Download the DMG, drag SaberDock into Applications, then follow the [user guide](https://github.com/Arch1xxxxx/SaberDock#readme). The app is locally signed and is not Apple-notarized. ZIP and SHA-256 checksums are also provided.

SaberDock includes and distributes no games, APKs, OBBs, paid music packs, or game-derived runtimes. It is a companion tool for custom content for games you already own on devices you already own. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

在 Mac 上整理 Beat Saber 自定义谱面，并使用你自己的 AVP Play 游戏文件将歌单同步至 Apple Vision Pro。

- 拖入谱面，按文件夹创建歌单。
- 浏览歌曲封面和难度，保留受支持谱面的配色。
- 删除、恢复、排列和排序歌曲。
- 一键更新头显曲库，自定义应用名称。
- 英文与简体中文界面。

**安装或更新前，须准备至少两个非空歌单，每个至少包含一份受支持的谱面。只有一个歌单时无法进入头显游戏主菜单。**

需要 macOS 14 或更新版本的 Apple Silicon Mac、带兼容 visionOS SDK 的 Xcode、已配对的 Vision Pro，以及通过 AVP Play 完成的 Beat Saber 1.40.8安装，并保留 Mac 上对应的 visionOS 应用构建。SaberDock 复用该应用，不自行转译或重新构建游戏可执行程序。

下载 DMG，将 SaberDock 拖入“应用程序”，再按照[使用指引](https://github.com/Arch1xxxxx/SaberDock#readme)设置。应用使用本地签名，尚未经过 Apple 公证。也提供 ZIP 和 SHA-256 校验文件。

SaberDock 不包含、也不分发游戏、APK、OBB、付费曲包或由游戏生成的运行文件，只用于在已有设备上游玩已拥有游戏的自定义内容。所有商标均属于其各自所有人。
